# Worked Example: A Fictional Job Search Outreach

An entirely fictional scenario. Reaching out cold to Dana Voss, an engineering manager at a fictional startup, Bramford Analytics.

## What You Are Trying to Achieve

Get a genuine conversation going about backend engineering roles at Bramford Analytics, ideally leading to a real application being seriously considered rather than lost in a general applicant pool.

## What Is Actually Known

- Bramford Analytics has posted three separate backend engineer job listings for the same team in the last month, confirmed directly on their own careers page
- Dana Voss is listed as the hiring manager on all three listings
- The sender has four years of experience specifically in the kind of data pipeline work the listings describe
- No prior contact with Dana or the company exists
